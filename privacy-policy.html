Privacy Policy

Developer / Studio: Cyrus Games Studio
Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Cyrus Games ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes mobile games for Android devices, including Grand Theft Money: Miami, Hill Climb 3D: Offroad Racing, and other titles published under our Google Play developer account (collectively, the "Games").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you download and play our Games. By installing or using our Games, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or directly provide personal information such as your name, home address, or phone number to play our Games. However, our Games use third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for game functionality, analytics, and advertising that may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, language settings, screen resolution, IP address, and general region/country (derived from IP address).
  • Advertising & App Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), app instance IDs, and crash/diagnostic identifiers.
  • Gameplay & Interaction Data: Session duration, game progress, ad impressions, ad clicks, and crash or performance logs.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected through our Games and third-party SDKs is used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve game stability, performance, and gameplay balance.
  • Diagnose technical issues, bugs, and application crashes.
  • Serve contextual or personalized advertisements to keep our Games free to play.
  • Measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our Games integrate third-party services that collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the third-party providers used in our Games:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising & Mediation): https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Unity & Unity Ads (Game Engine, Analytics & Advertising): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Mintegral (Advertising): https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy

4. Advertising & Opt-Out Choices

Our Games display third-party advertisements (such as banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads). Depending on your region and consent choices, ad networks may use your Google Advertising ID to serve personalized ads or non-personalized contextual ads.

How to opt out or reset your Advertising ID on Android:
Open your device Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads), where you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely. Where required by law (such as in the EEA/UK), you can also manage your consent preferences through the in-game consent prompt.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Cyrus Games does not store personal user profiles on proprietary servers. Technical, analytics, and advertising data collected by third-party SDKs is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in their respective privacy policies.

You can delete locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the Game. If you have questions regarding data deletion or wish to submit a privacy request, please contact us at the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our Games, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & Regional Rights)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, as well as the right to withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Games, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
